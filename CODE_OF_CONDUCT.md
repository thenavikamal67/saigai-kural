# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Saigai Kural project aims to provide a respectful and inclusive community for everyone interested in accessibility and sign-language technology.

## Expected Behaviour

- Treat all contributors with respect.
- Welcome different levels of experience.
- Respect Deaf people, ISL users, and the wider accessibility community.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on improving the project and its documentation.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Uploading unauthorised participant recordings.
- Misrepresenting dataset quality or model performance.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through a suitable contact channel. Do not publish sensitive personal information in public issues.

Project maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a respectful environment.
